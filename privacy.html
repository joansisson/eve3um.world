Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EvE3UM Software ("we", "us") handles information when you use our software applications, tools, and desktop products (collectively, the "Software").

1. Local-first by design

Our applications are local-first. Your data — your files, databases, projects, and settings — is stored on your own device in a local database. We do not see it, do not store it, and do not have access to it. You own your data, and it stays on your machine unless you choose to send it somewhere.

2. Information we collect

  • We do not require user accounts.
  • We do not run analytics or tracking. There is no telemetry, no advertising identifiers, and no usage tracking inside the Software.
  • Support contact: If you email us, we use your email address and the contents of your message only to respond to you.

3. Purchases and subscriptions

If you purchase any of our Software products or subscriptions through an application store (such as the Microsoft Store), the store platform processes the payment and handles your billing information on our behalf. Their handling of your information is governed by their respective privacy declarations, such as the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

4. License verification

To prevent unauthorized use, the Software periodically verifies your license status with our backend service. This check sends only the application version and a license token — no personal data, no user files, and no project content. If the verification check cannot reach our service, the Software continues to function offline without interruption.

5. On-Device AI features

Some features within our Software utilize Artificial Intelligence subsystems to categorize, extract, or generate content. These capabilities run natively on your local device via the Windows Foundry AI framework. Because processing occurs entirely on your local machine, your data, files, and project contents are never transmitted to us or any third-party cloud servers during AI operations.

6. Data security

We design our Software to keep your data secure on your device. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of information stored locally if your physical hardware device or local operating system environment is compromised.

7. Children's privacy

Our Software is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of our Software after changes means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at js.EvE3UM@programmer.net.
