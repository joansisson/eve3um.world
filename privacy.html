Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EvE3UM Software ("we", "us") handles information when you use our software, including the UDX desktop application and related products.

1. Local-first by design

Our applications are local-first. Your data — your files, databases, projects, and settings — is stored on your own device in a local database. We do not see it, do not store it, and do not have access to it. You own your data, and it stays on your machine unless you choose to send it somewhere.

2. Information we collect

  • We do not require an account.
  • We do not run analytics or tracking. There is no telemetry, no advertising identifiers, and no usage tracking.
  • Support contact: If you email us, we use your email address and the contents of your message only to respond to you.

3. Purchases and subscriptions

If you purchase UDX or a subscription through the Microsoft Store, Microsoft processes the payment and handles your billing information on our behalf. Their handling of your information is governed by the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

4. License verification

To prevent unauthorized use, the application periodically verifies your license with our backend service. This check sends only your application version and a license token — no personal data, no files, and no content from your projects. If the check cannot reach our service, the app continues to work offline without interruption.

5. Optional AI features

Some features use AI (such as Google Gemini) to categorize, extract, or generate content. These features are opt-in and require you to supply your own API key (Bring Your Own Key). When you use an AI feature, the content you choose to process may be sent to the AI provider for processing. That data is handled under the AI provider's privacy policy, such as the Google Privacy Policy. Your API key is stored encrypted on your device and is never shared by us.

6. Data security

We design our software to keep your data secure on your device, including encrypting sensitive values such as API keys. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of information transmitted over the internet.

7. Children's privacy

Our software is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of our software after changes means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at js.EvE3UM@programmer.net.
